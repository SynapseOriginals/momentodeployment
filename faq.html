Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about our guided legacy-preservation process, storyteller comfort, logistics, investment, and privacy.

Storyteller Comfort & The Experience

What if my parent or grandparent is nervous or feels their life isn't "interesting"?

This is the single most common concern families share with us. Older generations are naturally modest. Our Storyteller Preparation process gently reframes the entire experience: they are not giving a performance or sitting for an exam. They are simply having a warm, comfortable conversation with an attentive listener who knows how to honor their memories and life wisdom.

What is the Ethical Question Filter?

While family members can suggest themes and curiosities, Momento maintains a strict ethical boundary. Our Story Discovery Lead curates all prompts so the conversation remains empowering and respectful. We never ask questions that cause distress, confrontation, or emotional discomfort. Dignity is non-negotiable.

Where does the guided conversation take place?

We meet in the storyteller's natural home environment—their favorite living room armchair or study—or a quiet, familiar setting where they feel completely at ease.

How long does a filming session take?

Typically 2 to 3 hours of unhurried, comfortable conversation. We take frequent breaks and match the natural energy of the storyteller. The environment is kept calm, respectful, and discreet.

Logistics, Service Area & Turnaround

Where is Momento based, and do you travel?

Momento is based in Hyderabad. We regularly travel to conduct legacy preservation sessions across all major cities and towns in India (including Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, and beyond). International legacy projects are also undertaken by custom discussion.

What is the expected turnaround time for our film?

The complete journey from Story Discovery to final Private Delivery typically takes 3 to 5 weeks. If your family has an upcoming milestone—such as a 75th or 80th birthday, golden anniversary, or holiday gathering—we can coordinate our editing timeline to align with your date.

Investment & Pricing Transparency

How much does a Momento Legacy Film cost?

Investment starts from ₹1,50,000 depending on location, travel requirements, and story scope. We do not use confusing package catalogues or upsells. Pricing is discussed transparently and fixed during your initial consultation with zero hidden fees.

What is included in the preservation investment?

Your investment covers the complete 7-stage guided journey: family consultation, personalized story discovery framework, storyteller preparation, director-led home filming, thoughtful chaptered editing, internal Momento Quality Control, a private password-protected streaming portal for your family, and permanent redundant master file preservation.

Privacy, Consent & Family Ownership

Who owns the legacy film and master recordings?

Your family owns 100% of all master recordings and finished films. Momento never publishes, broadcasts, or uses your family's personal memories for marketing without explicit, separate written consent.

How does our family access the finished film?

You receive an encrypted, password-protected private digital link. There are no complicated app downloads or mandatory account registrations. You can share the link and passcode directly with immediate family members to watch together on TVs, tablets, or phones.

How are our master recordings preserved for future generations?

Behind your private streaming portal, master preservation archives are safely retained across redundant cloud storage vaults so your family always has a permanent place to return to their heritage.
