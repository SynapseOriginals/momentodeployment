Our Philosophy

Family Legacy Preservation, Not Ordinary Videography

What Momento Is Not

Most commercial videography focuses on equipment, artificial staging, and impersonal transactions where families are handed raw unedited video files or generic interview prompts.

  • Not an intimidating interrogation or stressful camera spotlight
  • Not a raw dump of disconnected video recordings
  • Not AI avatars, synthetic voices, or digital recreations
  • Not a social media channel or public broadcast
  • Not a high-pressure sales funnel

The Momento Standard

A gentle, unhurried human conversation led by compassionate directors who prioritize storyteller comfort, emotional dignity, and authentic family voice.

  • Guided Story Discovery: We identify the milestones and memories that truly matter.
  • Ethical Question Filter: A protective boundary ensuring parents and grandparents always feel safe.
  • Director-Led Conversation: The focus remains entirely on the person, not the medium.
  • Chaptered Legacy Heirloom: Structured into intuitive chapters for effortless rewatching.
  • Private & Permanent: Encrypted family viewing links with long-term redundant master preservation.
