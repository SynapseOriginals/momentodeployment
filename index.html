Our Philosophy

Family Legacy Preservation, Not Ordinary Videography

What Momento Is Not

Most commercial videography focuses on equipment, staging, and transactional shoots where families are handed raw unedited files or generic corporate interviews.

  • Not an intimidating interrogation or stressful camera set
  • Not a raw dump of 14 disjointed video files
  • Not AI avatars, synthetic voices, or digital clones
  • Not a social media network or public platform
  • Not a high-pressure sales funnel

The Momento Standard

A gentle, unscripted human conversation led by trained directors who prioritize comfort, emotional safety, and genuine authentic voice.

  • Guided Story Discovery: We identify the moments and turning points that matter.
  • Ethical Question Filter: An ethical firewall ensuring the storyteller feels safe and respected.
  • Director-Led Conversation: Focus remains on the person, not the camera.
  • Chaptered Cinematic Edit: Structured into meaningful life chapters.
  • Private & Permanent: Simple password-protected family access and master preservation.
The Momento Method

How We Preserve Your Family's Story

Discover → Prepare → Film → Craft → Preserve

01 Consult

1. Consultation & Fit

"Building trust before cameras ever roll."

We speak directly with you to understand who the storyteller is, your family context, timeline, and any sensitive subjects. We confirm whether the storyteller is willing and comfortable before proceeding.

No high-pressure sales Family context discovery Comfort qualification
02 Discover

2. Story Discovery & Ethical Filter

"A personalized story structure, never a generic questionnaire."

Your family shares memories, relationships, milestones, and topics to explore or avoid. Our Story Discovery Lead structures these into a natural arc while applying our strict Ethical Question Filter to prevent confrontational or anxiety-inducing moments.

Curated chapter outline Ethical question firewall Sensitive-topic safeguards
03 Prepare

3. Storyteller Preparation

"You're coming to have a conversation, not to be interviewed."

Older storytellers often fear their lives aren't interesting enough. We ease performance anxiety through warm, personal conversations ahead of filming, creating an atmosphere of validation and safety.

Anxiety reduction Warm personal briefing Zero memorization needed
04 Film

4. Director-Led Filming

"Person → Conversation → Story → Emotion."

Conducted in the storyteller's comfortable space with a discreet, respectful crew. The director guides the narrative naturally while our camera operator captures studio-grade audio and cinema visuals without intruding on the moment.

Discreet multi-angle capture Studio-grade acoustics Unscripted, organic flow
05 Preserve

5. Chaptered Craft, QC & Private Delivery

"A private heirloom your family can return to for decades."

Our editors transform the footage into a chaptered legacy film, rigorously reviewed for narrative integrity and audio-visual excellence. Delivered through a simple, password-protected private link—with reliable master file backups retained.

Internal Momento Quality Control Password-gated private viewing Master redundancy & preservation
